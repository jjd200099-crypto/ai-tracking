Key Takeaways
AI labs can be wildly profitable. Anthropic turns GAAP-profitable in 3Q26E ($1.2B EBIT, 6% margin) while growing NNARR faster than OpenAI, which sits at roughly -100% EBIT margins. The business model is the moat: 75–85% of revenue is usage-based API (subscriptions just 15%, consumer only 5%), driving blended gross margin from -94% in 2024 to ~65% in 2026 (API GM 80%+). Reinvestment flywheel: 2027E revenue-after-COGS of $160B vs OpenAI's $92B, and a cumulative EBTIT advantage of ~$250B through 2028, funds the next SOTA model and widens the lead. Valuation: base case 20× 2027E ending ARR of $300B = $6T enterprise value; requires NNARR accelerating to ~$15B/month in 2027 (vs $10B+/mo today).
